Sunday began without an alarm. The morning light arrived before I did, and for once there was nowhere I needed to hurry.

## Leaving space in the day

I made coffee, opened the back door and sat outside while it was still cool. Usually I would use that time to plan everything that needed doing. Instead, I listened to the garden waking up.

Later there was a walk, a good lunch and a book left open on the sofa. None of it was remarkable. That was exactly why it felt so restorative.

> Rest does not have to be earned by first becoming exhausted.

I am writing this down as a reminder: an unhurried day is not an empty one.
