Some weeks do not contain a grand adventure or a life-changing revelation. They can still be full of moments worth keeping.

## This week's little list

- The smell of rain through an open window.
- A neighbour leaving flowers by the gate.
- Finding the exact book I wanted in a charity shop.
- The first perfectly ripe blackberry of the year.
- A voice note from an old friend.

Noticing these things does not mean ignoring the difficult parts. It simply gives the good moments somewhere to land.

Next week, I am going to keep a scrap of paper nearby and see what else makes the list.
